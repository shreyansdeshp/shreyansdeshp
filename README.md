### 🛰️ Featured Projects

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**[LiDAR — Archaeological Site Detection](https://github.com/shreyansdeshp/LiDAR)**
`PyTorch` `ConvNeXt` `QGIS` `Rasterio`

Multimodal deep-learning pipeline fusing 1m LiDAR terrain data with Sentinel-2 imagery to detect archaeological sites across 226 km² of Ireland. Adapted ConvNeXt-Tiny to 11-channel multispectral input — 72% accuracy, 0.775 ROC-AUC — and generated a georeferenced probability heatmap narrowing field survey to 24% of the area.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**JalSETU — Dam Break Inundation Modelling**
`Python` `DualSPHysics` `Delft3D` `GEE`

Automated dam-break flood pipeline coupling SPH breach simulation with Delft3D FM downstream routing, cutting setup time from days to ~10 minutes per run. Simulated a full Ukai Dam breach: 2,290 km² flooded, 59.2 lakh people at risk, validated against Sentinel-1 SAR flood masks.

</td>
